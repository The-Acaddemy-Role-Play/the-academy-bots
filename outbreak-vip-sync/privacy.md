# Outbreak VIP Sync — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026  
**Operator:** The Academy Servers Development  
**Discord Application ID:** `1465941522951311527`

## 1. Introduction

Outbreak VIP Sync (the **Bot**) is a proprietary Discord application operated exclusively by **The Academy Servers Development (TAS)**.

The Bot is intended solely for authorized use within The Academy Servers ecosystem, including designated Outbreak, Partnered, and Ambassador Discord communities approved by TAS.

The Bot synchronizes designated VIP membership tiers and eligible add-on roles between participating Discord servers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, how it is used, what synchronization activity is logged, and how individuals may submit privacy-related requests.

The Bot is not offered as a publicly available service, and participation in its synchronization system is restricted to Discord servers expressly authorized by TAS.

## 2. Information Processed

The Bot processes information obtained through Discord as necessary for synchronization, including:

- **Discord user IDs:** to identify the same member across participating servers.
- **Usernames:** to identify members in synchronization reports and administrative logs.
- **Server IDs, role IDs, and member-role information:** to determine role eligibility and apply appropriate role mappings.
- **Command information:** to recognize the `!syncvip` command and the account invoking it.
- **Synchronization records:** user ID, username, VIP tier, source server, actions, initiating user, and timestamp, where applicable.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

This information is used to check VIP eligibility, synchronize qualifying roles, process authorized commands, produce administrative synchronization logs, diagnose problems, and protect the service from misuse. It is not used for behavioral advertising, commercial data sales, or unrelated profiling.

## 4. Message Content

The Bot uses Discord's **Message Content** intent to recognize the `!syncvip` command. Its intended purpose is command processing, not general message monitoring, archiving ordinary conversations, or reading private messages.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

In the previously reviewed implementation, Discord membership and role information is processed in application memory for synchronization, **without a separate member database or JSON-based persistent member store**.

The Bot does, however, send synchronization activity to a Discord webhook in a designated administrative logging channel. Such messages may include user IDs, usernames, VIP tiers, source servers, actions, initiating users, and timestamps. These records remain in Discord channel history until deleted, subject to Discord's own practices and any applicable retention requirements. The operator may delete logs when no longer needed and will review valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable law. A fixed automatic deletion period is not represented or guaranteed here.

## 6. Access and Sharing

Access to synchronization records is restricted to authorized personnel of The Academy Servers Development and other expressly authorized administrators who require access for operational, technical, or administrative purposes.

Synchronization logs are maintained in designated Discord logging channels. Access to these records is governed by the permissions assigned to those channels.

Information is transmitted through Discord's API and the hosting infrastructure necessary to operate the Bot.

The Bot does not make member information publicly available through its synchronization functions.

The Academy Servers Development does not sell personal information, provide it to advertisers or data brokers, or use it for unrelated commercial profiling.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to investigate security incidents, prevent unauthorized access, or protect the integrity of the Bot and participating communities.

## 7. Security

The Academy Servers Development maintains reasonable technical and administrative safeguards intended to protect information processed by the Bot.

Access to Bot credentials, Discord webhook credentials, hosting infrastructure, and administrative synchronization logs is restricted to authorized personnel.

The Bot's synchronization functions are intended for authorized Discord communities and designated administrators.

No online service can guarantee absolute security, and these safeguards do not eliminate all potential risks.

## 8. Access and Deletion Requests

To ask about your information or request deletion of applicable Bot-controlled synchronization log entries:

1. Join the [official support server](https://discord.gg/officermesser).
2. Open the [support ticket channel](https://discord.com/channels/810199864264097843/838081486502494239).
3. Submit a ticket identifying the request as a privacy, access, or deletion request.
4. Provide your Discord user ID if requested to locate relevant records.

The operator may verify control of the account before disclosing or deleting information. Requests will be evaluated under applicable law, with any relevant security or legal retention exceptions. Deleting Bot-controlled information does not necessarily delete data independently held by Discord.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for Discord users who meet the minimum age requirements applicable to their jurisdiction. It is not directed at younger children and is not intended to knowingly collect their personal information contrary to law.

## 10. Third-Party Services

The Bot uses Discord's platform and API. Discord independently processes account information under the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). The Bot may also use hosting infrastructure required to operate.
