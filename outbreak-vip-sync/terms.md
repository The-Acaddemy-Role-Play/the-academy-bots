## 2. Description of Service

The Bot synchronizes designated VIP membership tiers and eligible add-on roles between authorized Partnered, Ambassador, and Outbreak Discord servers. The `!syncvip` command initiates synchronization. The Bot may identify members using Discord user IDs, check qualifying roles, determine corresponding VIP tiers, grant eligible roles, and log actions for administrative review.

**The reviewed implementation is grant-only:** it grants qualifying roles but does not automatically revoke VIP roles during synchronization.
