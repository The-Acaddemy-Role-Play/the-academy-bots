## 2. Description of Service

Outbreak VIP Sync is a proprietary Discord application designed to synchronize designated VIP membership tiers and eligible add-on roles between authorized Partnered, Ambassador, and Outbreak Discord servers.

The `!syncvip` command initiates synchronization.

The Bot's functionality includes:

- Identifying members using their unique Discord user IDs.
- Checking qualifying membership and VIP roles across participating servers.
- Determining corresponding VIP tiers and eligible add-on roles.
- Granting qualifying roles to eligible members.
- Recording synchronization activity for administrative review and troubleshooting.

**The Bot operates as a grant-only synchronization system.** It grants qualifying roles but does not automatically revoke existing VIP roles during synchronization.
